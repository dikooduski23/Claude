But here's the twist. Jamukha, the friend who saved your wife... becomes your greatest enemy. He favors noble families. You do something crazy for the twelfth century. You promote by talent, not by blood. A herder's son can become a general. In one battle, an enemy archer shoots your war horse through the neck. After you win, you ask the prisoners... who shot my horse? One man steps forward. I did. Kill me, or spare me and I'll serve you. You spare him. You name him Jebe... the arrow. He becomes one of your greatest generals. That's why men leave Jamukha... and follow you. Years later, Jamukha's own men hand him over. You offer him his life. He refuses. The old chronicle says he asked for a noble death, without spilling his blood. And you granted it.

Spring, twelve-oh-six. The tribes gather. And the boy everyone abandoned is named ruler of them all. Genghis Khan. You break up the old clans into units of ten, a hundred, a thousand. You give your people a code of law. You allow every religion. You start horse relay stations, which your son will turn into a network across the empire. Then you ride. In twelve fifteen, you take the Jin capital, where Beijing stands today. In twelve eighteen, you send about four hundred fifty merchants west, to trade with the Khwarazm Empire. The governor of Otrar calls them spies... and kills them all. One camel driver escapes. You send envoys to the Shah for justice. He beheads one, and shaves the beards of the others. Within three years... his empire collapses. Cities are wiped out. Your armies cross mountains no one thought they could cross. This is the part of your story the world never forgets. And the man who started it... has only a few years left.

Twelve twenty-seven. You're in your sixties, at war again, against the Western Xia. And in August... you die. How? Nobody knows for sure. Every story about it was written later. Even your grave is a mystery. Nearly eight hundred years later, no one has found it. In two thousand three, a study claimed one in two hundred men alive today might descend from you. Newer research says that bloodline... might not be yours at all. But the empire you started became the largest connected land empire in history.
