## Description
You're born on the freezing Mongolian steppe, clutching a clot of blood. By nine your father is poisoned. Your clan abandons you. You kill your half-brother over a fish and end up as a prisoner in a wooden collar. Then you become Genghis Khan.

This is the full life of Temujin, told as if it were yours, from the old chronicle "The Secret History of the Mongols" and modern historians.

Who should be born next: Nikola Tesla, a Spartan, a Roman gladiator or a child of Pompeii? Tell me in the comments.
