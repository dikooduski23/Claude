## YouTube description
```
What if Earth had a ring like Saturn? It might have. A 2024 study found that 21 impact craters from about 466 million years ago all formed near the equator of the time, even though most land was elsewhere. The researchers suggest a passing asteroid was torn apart by Earth's gravity and formed a debris ring, and that its shadow may have helped cool the planet into the Hirnantian ice age. It's a hypothesis, still being tested.

Sources: Tomkins et al., Earth and Planetary Science Letters 646 (2024); Monash University; The Conversation (A. Tomkins); Space.com; Smithsonian Magazine; EOS.
```
