## YouTube description
Over 2,000 years ago, Hannibal commanded the fleet of King Prusias I of Bithynia against the bigger navy of Eumenes II of Pergamon. His trick was to have his men collect venomous snakes, seal them in clay jars, and throw the jars onto the enemy decks. The enemy laughed… until the snakes got out.
