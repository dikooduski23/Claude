## YouTube description
```
In 1796 Count Rumford made fireplaces smaller and the rooms got warmer. The trick: a fire heats you with radiant heat, while the hot air just escapes up the chimney with the smoke. His shallow fireplace with angled sides bounced the heat into the room, and a narrow throat shot the smoke straight up. Over 150 London fireplaces were rebuilt his way in two months.

Sources: Count Rumford, "Of Chimney Fire-places" (1796); Jane Austen, Northanger Abbey (1817); JASNA Persuasions On-Line 23.1 (2002); Buckley Rumford Co.; This Old House.
```
