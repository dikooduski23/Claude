## YouTube description
Romanian-born economist Stefan Mandel won the lottery 14 times by spotting games where the jackpot was worth more than the cost of buying every possible number combination.

In February 1992, Virginia's jackpot reached about $27 million, while every combination (7,059,052 of them) cost about $7 million. His syndicate printed millions of tickets in a Melbourne warehouse with 30 computers and 12 laser printers, then couriers bought them at stores across Virginia. Time ran out with roughly 700,000 combinations unbought, but the winning ticket was already in their pile.

The FBI, CIA and other agencies investigated, and no wrongdoing was found. Australia had already banned one person covering every combination, and US lottery states later passed laws blocking the strategy.
