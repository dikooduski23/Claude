How was the Leaning Tower of Pisa saved? They dug under it 🏗️

In 1990 it leaned so far that Pisa closed it. Lead weights didn't fix it. Freezing the ground made it lean even more.

Then engineer John Burland had them pull soil out from under the high side, a little at a time, and the Pisa tower tipped back about 40 cm.
