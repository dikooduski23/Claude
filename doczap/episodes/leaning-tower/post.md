## YouTube description
How was the Leaning Tower of Pisa saved? In 1990 it leaned so far that it was closed to the public. Hundreds of tonnes of lead were stacked on its north side, and freezing the ground for new anchors in 1995 made it lean even more. Engineer John Burland proposed the fix: drill under the high (north) side and remove small amounts of soil, a little at a time. The tower settled back by about 40 centimetres, and it reopened in December 2001.
