Captain Cook got his crew eating sauerkraut by serving it only at the officers' table. The true story of Cook's reverse psychology trick on HMB Endeavour, written in his own journal.

In 1768 the Endeavour sailed with nearly 8,000 pounds of sauerkraut to fight scurvy, but the sailors wouldn't touch it. Cook had it served every day to his officers and left it optional for the men. Within a week he had to put the whole ship on a ration. On his next voyage, three years at sea, no one on his ship died of scurvy.

Sources: Cook's Endeavour journal, 13 April 1769 (National Library of Australia MS1; State Library of NSW transcript); Hektoen International (2021); State Library of NSW and National Museum of Australia on the Resolution voyage.
