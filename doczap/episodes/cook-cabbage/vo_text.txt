Captain Cook got his crew eating stinky pickled cabbage... by feeding it to his officers.
Scurvy rotted sailors' gums... and killed thousands.
Cook packed nearly eight thousand pounds of sauerkraut... and the sailors wouldn't touch it.
So Cook tried a trick.
Every day, his officers got a plate. The sailors? Only if they wanted.
Within a week... Cook had to put the ship on a ration.
You see, sailors grumble at anything new.
But let the bosses value it... and it's "the finest stuff in the world."
His next voyage: three years at sea... no one on his ship died of scurvy.
He didn't sell cabbage... he sold jealousy.
